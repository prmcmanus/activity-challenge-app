Quick answers

How do challenges work?

A challenge runs between two dates and measures either active minutes or distance (miles or km). It's played in teams or by individuals. Join one with the invite code its owner shares; a team code joins you to that team and its challenge in one go.

How do I log activity?

Open a challenge and use Log activity. Enter what the challenge measures (minutes or distance); the other is optional. You can attach a GPX file to save the route and fill in the distance and times. The date must fall within the challenge. Edit or delete your entries under My recent activity.

Can my phone sync workouts automatically?

Yes, with the Android app: it reads workouts from Health Connect (Google Fit, Samsung Health, Strava and others write there). Review them before uploading, or switch on Sync automatically under Me. One workout counts in every challenge it fits. An iPhone app is planned.

Why didn't a workout count in my distance challenge?

It probably has no recorded distance (yoga or gym sessions, for example). In the app you can type the distance during review; on the web, edit the entry and add it.

Who can see my profile and activity?

Only people in a challenge with you, and only for challenges you share. Choose Private, Totals or Full under My account. GPS routes are only ever visible to you. See the Privacy Policy.

How long is data kept, and can I delete it?

A challenge and everything in it is deleted 60 days after it ends, or earlier if its owner deletes it. You can delete any entry you logged. To delete your account, send a ticket and we'll take care of it.
