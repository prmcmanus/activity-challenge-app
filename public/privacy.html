Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-06.

Active Together is a small, self-hosted app for running team activity challenges. This page explains what data it holds, who can see it, and how long it keeps it. It's written in plain language rather than legal boilerplate, because that's a better match for what this app actually is: a tool a small group of people use together, not a commercial product.

What we collect

  • Account: the name and email address you register with, and a salted, hashed copy of your password — never the password itself.
  • Activity entries: activity type, duration in minutes and/or distance, date, an optional start/finish time, and an optional comment you write yourself.
  • Support tickets: bug reports, feature requests and questions you send from Help - the title, details, an optional screenshot, your replies, and the device/app version or browser they came from. Only you and the site's administrators can see them.
  • Uploaded images: an optional profile avatar, an optional team logo, and any images you insert into a challenge description.
  • Challenge/team content: challenge and team names, descriptions, and your membership/role in them.
  • Follows: who you follow and who follows you.
  • Device sync data (Android/iOS companion apps only): if you use a companion app, it reads workout duration, date, type and start/finish time, plus the distance covered during each workout if you allow it, from Health Connect or Apple Health and uploads only that. It never reads or uploads heart rate, calories, or any other health data, and never reads records beyond exercise/workout sessions, the distance recorded during them and - only if you turn it on - their routes.
  • Apple Shortcuts sync (optional): if you set it up, a shortcut on your iPhone sends your daily totals of steps, exercise minutes and walking and running distance from Apple Health, and nothing else. It uses a personal sync key that we store only in scrambled (hashed) form; you can remove it at any time under Set up Apple Shortcuts on the website.
  • GPS routes (optional): a workout's route - a series of map positions with times - is stored only if you upload a GPX file on the website or switch on "Include GPS routes" in the Android app. A route is precise location data and can show where you live or work, so it is off by default, it is visible only to you (never to other members, team admins, challenge owners or in exports), and it is deleted along with the last activity entry that uses it.

Who can see what

Challenges and teams are private by default — nobody can see a challenge, its teams, its members, or its leaderboard unless they've joined it with an invite code or been added directly. Within a challenge you're a member of:

  • Anyone in the challenge can see its team and individual leaderboards, including names and avatars.
  • Your profile (name, photo and an optional "about me" line) can be opened by people who share a challenge with you, and shows only challenges you share. What else it shows is your choice in My account: Private (nothing more), Totals (your total and rank in each shared challenge - the default) or Full (also your recent activity in those challenges, with comments).
  • Anyone who can open your profile sees how many followers you have and how many people you follow. Unless your profile is Private, they also see who those people are, but only the ones they share a challenge with themselves. You can only follow someone you share a challenge with.
  • Your GPS routes are visible only to you.
  • Anyone in a team can see that team's member list (names and emails).
  • Only a challenge owner or a global admin can export leaderboard data as a CSV file, or add another owner.

A global admin (the person who runs this server) can access account records for user administration — creating accounts, resetting passwords, and similar housekeeping.

Third parties

If reCAPTCHA is enabled on this deployment, Google receives your IP address and browser information when you register or sign in, in order to distinguish humans from bots. No other activity or account data is shared with Google or anyone else. This app sends no analytics or tracking data to any third party.

Data retention

A challenge — and everything scoped to it (its teams, memberships, activity entries and logged minutes or distance) — is automatically and permanently deleted 60 days after the challenge's end date. This runs on a daily schedule with no manual step required, and cannot be undone once it happens. If you want to keep a record of a challenge's results, export the leaderboard CSVs (or take your own copy) before that window closes.

A challenge's owner can also delete it at any time before then, which removes the same data immediately and permanently, for every member.

You can leave a challenge at any time. Everything you logged in it is deleted straight away. Leaving just a team keeps what you logged under it on the team's total.

Your account itself is not deleted when a challenge is purged — only the challenge-specific data is. Your account, and any other challenges you belong to, are unaffected.

Images you've uploaded (avatars, team logos, description images) are not automatically cleaned up when a challenge is purged — this is a known gap, not a promise that they're removed. If you want an uploaded image removed, ask the server administrator.

Account deletion

You can delete your account yourself at any time: on the website under My account, or in the Android or iPhone app under Me, then Edit. It needs your password, and it immediately and permanently deletes your account, everything you've logged, your routes, your follows and your support tickets. A challenge you own on your own passes to its longest-standing member, or is deleted if nobody else is in it. If you can't sign in any more, ask the person administering this server and they'll delete it for you.

Security

Passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text), sessions are HTTP-only cookies, and rich-text challenge descriptions are filtered server-side to strip anything that could run as code. This is a small, self-hosted app run in good faith, not an audited enterprise product — treat it accordingly and don't put anything genuinely sensitive into it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters (for example, a change to the retention window), the "last updated" date at the top will change. There's no mailing list or notification system to announce it beyond that.
