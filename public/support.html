Support

Questions, problems or ideas: we're happy to help.

Get in touch

  • Signed in? Use Help on the website (in the app: the ? at the top of the main screens) to send a ticket. You'll see our replies there.
  • Can't sign in, or rather email? Write to support@activetogether.team.

Quick answers

How do I join a challenge?

Use the invite link or code someone shared with you. On the website, tap Join with a code; in the app, Join with code. New to Active Together? Create an account at activetogether.team first.

How do I sync my workouts and steps?

On Android, the app reads Health Connect; on iPhone, the app or the Apple Shortcut reads Apple Health. See Sync from your phone.

Who can see my activity?

Only people in a challenge with you, and only for challenges you share. GPS routes are only ever visible to you. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

On the website, My account, then Delete my account; in the app, Me, then Edit, then Delete my account. It needs your password and deletes everything straight away. If you can't sign in, email support@activetogether.team from the address on your account.
