Sync from your phone

Count your workouts and steps without typing them in. You set it up once. Got a watch? It works through your phone, see the tips below.

Android

  1. On your phone, sign in at activetogether.team and tap Get the app under "Sync from your phone".
  2. Open the download. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.
  3. Open Active Together, sign in, tap Sync and allow Health Connect.
  4. Under Me, turn on Sync automatically.
Fitbit, Garmin, Samsung or another watch? In its app's settings, turn on sharing with Health Connect.

iPhone

Use Apple Shortcuts: it's free, already on your iPhone, and sends your day's steps, exercise minutes and distance twice a day.

  1. On your iPhone, sign in at activetogether.team and tap Set up under "Sync from your phone".
  2. Tap Get the shortcut, then Add Shortcut.
  3. Back on the website, tap Connect this iPhone. Allow access to Health and to activetogether.team when asked.
  4. Make it automatic: in Shortcuts, tap Automation, +, Time of Day. Choose 21:00, Daily and Run Immediately, then pick Active Together sync. Add a second one for 08:00.
Garmin? In Garmin Connect, turn on sharing with Apple Health. Fitbit? Fitbit doesn't share with Apple Health, so log your activity by hand.
Prefer an app that syncs each workout?

There's an iPhone app, installed from a computer with the free tool Sideloadly:

  1. Download the app on a Windows PC or Mac (signed in), and install Sideloadly (on Windows, also iTunes).
  2. Connect your iPhone with a cable, drag the downloaded file into Sideloadly, enter your Apple ID and press Start.
  3. On the iPhone: Settings, General, VPN & Device Management, your Apple ID, Trust. If asked, also turn on Developer Mode under Privacy & Security.

With a free Apple ID it needs installing again every 7 days.

Good to know

  • Use one way per challenge. Two ways (say, the app and the shortcut) would count the same activity twice.
  • Nothing came through? Open the app, or run the shortcut, with your phone unlocked. iPhones keep Health data locked while the phone is locked.
  • To stop: turn off Sync automatically in the app, or delete the automations in Shortcuts. What's already synced stays until you delete it.
