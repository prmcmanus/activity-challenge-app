Sync your activity automatically

You can always log activity by hand on the website. To have it counted for you, connect your phone. Your watch or fitness band doesn't need its own connection: its app passes everything to your phone's health store (Health Connect on Android, Apple Health on iPhone), and Active Together picks it up from there.

Use one way per challenge. If two of these send the same activity (say, the app and the shortcut), it counts twice. Pick one, or delete the duplicates under My activity.

Android: the Active Together app

  1. Sign in on this website, and on the home page tap Download for Android. Open the downloaded file to install it (allow installs from your browser if Android asks).
  2. Open the app, sign in, go to Sync and allow access to Health Connect when asked.
  3. Review your workouts and upload them, or turn on Sync automatically under Me.

With a Fitbit

In the Fitbit app (now called Google Health on some phones): tap your profile picture, then Health Connect, and turn sharing on for steps, exercise and distance.

With a Garmin (Android 14 or newer)

In Garmin Connect: More (or your profile) → Settings → Health Connect, and allow it to write steps, activities and distance.

With a Samsung, Pixel Watch or other app

Most fitness apps have a Health Connect option in their settings. Turn on writing exercise, steps and distance.

iPhone: Apple Shortcuts

Shortcuts is a free app from Apple, already on your iPhone (search for "Shortcuts" if you can't see it). You set this up once. After that it sends your day's steps, exercise minutes and walking and running distance from Apple Health twice a day, so it counts in step, minutes and distance challenges alike. It sends daily totals, not individual workouts.

Works with an Apple Watch, an iPhone on its own, or a Garmin. In Garmin Connect, go to More → Settings → Connected Apps → Apple Health and switch sharing on.

Step 1: get your sync key

  1. On this website, sign in and choose Set up Apple Shortcuts on the home page.
  2. Tap Create my sync key and Copy. It's shown only once. If you lose it, just make a new one; the old one then stops working.

If someone has shared a ready-made Active Together shortcut with you, tap their link, choose Add Shortcut, paste your key when asked, and go straight to step 3.

Step 2: build the shortcut

In the Shortcuts app, tap + at the top right. Tap the name at the top and call it Active Together sync. Then add these actions in order, using the search bar at the bottom ("Search for apps and actions") to find each one.

  1. Text: paste your sync key into the box.
  2. Set Variable: tap Variable Name and type Key. Its input should already be Text.

Steps (actions 3 to 9):

  1. Find Health Samples: tap Type and choose Steps. Tap Add Filter, set it to Start Date is in the last 2 days. Tap Show More and set Group By to Day.
  2. Repeat with Each: it should repeat with each item in Health Samples. Add the next actions inside it, above End Repeat.
  3. Get Details of Health Sample: set it to get Start Date of Repeat Item.
  4. Format Date: tap Date Format and choose Custom, then type yyyy-MM-dd as the format.
  5. Get Details of Health Sample: set it to get Value of Repeat Item.
  6. Get Contents of URL: type https://activetogether.team/api/shortcut/day as the URL, then tap Show More:
    • Method: POST
    • Headers: tap Add new header. Key X-Sync-Key; for the value, choose the Key variable (tap the box, then Select Variable).
    • Request Body: JSON. Tap Add new field, then Text: key date, value the Formatted Date. Add another Number field: key steps, value the Value from action 7.
  7. Check that End Repeat comes after action 8.

Exercise minutes (actions 10 to 16): repeat actions 3 to 9 (long-press each and choose Duplicate, then drag them below End Repeat), with two changes:

  • In Find Health Samples, choose the type Exercise Minutes.
  • In Get Contents of URL, rename the field steps to minutes, and point every Repeat Item, Formatted Date and Value at the actions in this copy.

Distance (actions 17 to 24): repeat actions 3 to 9 once more, with these changes:

  • In Find Health Samples, choose Walking + Running Distance.
  • Add one more Get Details of Health Sample inside the repeat, getting the Unit of Repeat Item.
  • In Get Contents of URL, call the number field distance, and add a Text field distance_unit whose value is that Unit.

Tap Done. Now tap the shortcut once to try it. The first time, iPhone asks to let Shortcuts read your Health data (allow Steps, Exercise Minutes and Walking + Running Distance) and to connect to activetogether.team (tap Always Allow). Then check My activity on this website: you'll see entries marked "shortcut" for yesterday and today.

If your steps look a little higher than in the Health app, check that each Find Health Samples has Group By: Day. That makes Apple Health count an Apple Watch and an iPhone together only once.

Step 3: run it automatically

  1. In Shortcuts, tap Automation at the bottom, then + (or New Automation).
  2. Choose Time of Day, pick 21:00 and Daily, and choose Run Immediately (turn off Notify When Run if you like). Tap Next and choose Active Together sync.
  3. Add a second automation the same way for 08:00. The morning run finishes off yesterday's totals.

iPhone keeps Health data locked while the phone is locked, so a run while it's locked sends nothing. That's harmless: the next run sends both yesterday and today again, and sending a day twice just updates it.

Share it with your challenge

Once it works, you can save everyone else steps 1 and 2. Tap the ⌄ next to the shortcut's name, then the ⓘ details, then Setup, and add an Import Question on the Text action ("Paste your Active Together sync key"). Remove your own key from the Text box first. Then use Share → Copy iCloud Link and send the link round. Each person pastes their own key when they add it.

iPhone: the Active Together app

The iPhone app syncs individual workouts (with their type, times and, if you choose, the route) as well as steps. It isn't in the App Store yet, so it's installed from a computer with the free tool Sideloadly. With a free Apple Account the installed app has to be refreshed every 7 days, which Sideloadly can do automatically. See Get the iPhone app on the home page.

Stopping

  • Android: turn off Sync automatically under Me, or uninstall the app.
  • Shortcuts: delete the automations (and the shortcut), or remove your sync key on the website so it can't send anything.

Anything already synced stays in your challenges until you delete it under My activity.
